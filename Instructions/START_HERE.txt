TESTING APP - START HERE

Open this app in Chrome or Edge:
http://localhost:3000

1. Double-click "Start Testing app.cmd".
2. Enter exactly one character.
3. Click "Connect ESP32".
4. Select the ESP32 USB-SERIAL CH340 port.
5. Press the physical button.

IMPORTANT
- This address is local to this computer only.
- Other computers need the app hosted online with an HTTPS address.
- Each person needs Chrome or Edge and their own ESP32 connected by USB.
